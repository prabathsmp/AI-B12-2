## 3. Market Gap
The market gap is the lack of a dedicated service that offers healthy, millet-based meal boxes specifically for office workers in Coimbatore. While there are various meal delivery services, none focus on the specific combination of healthy millet-based meals and direct delivery to office locations.

## 4. Recommended Positioning
"Deliver fresh, healthy millet meal boxes to your office for ₹200 per day. Enjoy professional, doctor-designed meals with no minimum order, direct to your desk."

- **Pricing Logic**: We price at ₹200 per day, which is ₹10 more than Mummy's Cloud Kitchen's price per meal. This price point is competitive and reflects the added value of direct office delivery, professional meal design, and the convenience of a dedicated service.
- **Why It Wins**:
  - **Direct Office Delivery**: Unlike Mummy's Cloud Kitchen and others, our service ensures the meal is delivered directly to your office, saving you time and effort.
  - **Professional Design**: Our meals are doctor-designed to ensure they meet health standards, providing peace of mind.
  - **No Minimum Order**: Flexibility in ordering without the need for a minimum order, making it suitable for both individuals and small teams.
