# Product Support and Warranty FAQ

## Product Warranty Coverage
* All electronic and motorized consumer appliances include a standard 1-year limited manufacturer warranty covering mechanical failure and factory defects.
* Warranty excludes damages caused by accidental drops, water submersion (unless certified IP68 water-resistant), normal wear and tear, or unauthorized third-party repairs.

## Broken or Missing Parts
* If an item arrives with missing parts, hardware, or attachments, contact support within 7 business days of delivery.
* We ship out replacement components free of charge via 2-day priority courier.

## Product Care and Maintenance
* Keep all electrical appliances clean and dry when not in use.
* For kitchen items like blenders, food processors, and juicers: stainless steel blades and glass jars are top-rack dishwasher safe; motor bases must only be wiped with a damp cloth and never submerged in liquids.

## Technical Compatibility
* All 120V household appliances are manufactured for North American standard grounded outlets.
* Dual-voltage (100V-240V) electronics support international usage with standard physical plug travel adapters.

## Gift Cards and B2B Bulk Orders
* Gift cards are available for individual consumer purchase in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $200 directly through the web checkout.
* We currently do not offer enterprise-level custom-branded corporate gift card programs or bulk B2B invoicing through the standard consumer portal.
