# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Processing and Dispatch
* Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST (Monday through Friday) are processed on the same business day.
* Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST, on weekends, or during public holidays are dispatched on the next business day.

## Shipping Methods and Delivery Timelines
* **Standard Ground Shipping:** 3 to 5 business days ($4.99 flat rate, free for orders above $50.00).
* **Expedited Shipping:** 2 business days ($12.99 flat rate).
* **Overnight Delivery:** Next business day by 7:00 PM ($24.99 flat rate).
* All delivery estimates begin from the date of dispatch, not the order placement date.

## Tracking Orders
* Customers receive an automated confirmation email with a tracking number and courier link within 4 hours of dispatch.
* Real-time package location updates typically populate on the carrier website within 12 to 24 hours of label creation.

## Delayed or Lost Shipments
* If a package has not arrived within 2 business days past the maximum estimated delivery window, customers are entitled to an investigation with the carrier.
* If a shipment shows no carrier movement for more than 7 consecutive business days, our support team will issue a free replacement or a full refund upon request.

## Damaged in Transit
* Items physically damaged during transit must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence of the damaged package and contents.
