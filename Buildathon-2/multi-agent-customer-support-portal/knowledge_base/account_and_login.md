# Account Management and Login Troubleshooting

## Password Reset Instructions
1. Navigate to the login page and click the **"Forgot Password?"** link.
2. Enter the verified email address associated with your store profile and click **"Send Reset Link"**.
3. Check your inbox (including Spam/Junk folders) for an email from `support@store.com` containing a secure 6-digit one-time PIN or direct link.
4. The password reset token expires exactly 15 minutes after issuance.
5. Create a new password containing at least 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one digit, and one special character (`!@#$%^&*`).

## Locked Accounts
* Accounts are automatically locked for 30 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts to protect against unauthorized access.
* To bypass the lockout immediately, use the **"Forgot Password?"** workflow to reset credentials.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
* If enabled, enter the 6-digit code generated by your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator).
* If you lost access to your 2FA device, utilize your emergency 16-character backup recovery codes generated during initial enrollment.
* If backup codes are lost, manual identity verification via human support is mandatory.

## Updating Profile and Email Address
* You can change your name, shipping address, and phone number directly from **Account Settings** > **Personal Details**.
* For security purposes, changing the account email address requires re-entering your current password and verifying confirmation links sent to both the old and new email addresses.
