# Returns and Refund Policy

## Return Eligibility Window
* Items may be returned within 30 calendar days from the official delivery date.
* To qualify for a full refund, items must be in original condition, unused, unwashed, and in the original packaging with all tags, seals, and accessories intact.

## Non-Returnable and Non-Refundable Items
* Perishable goods (food, supplements, flowers).
* Personalized, engraved, or custom-manufactured merchandise.
* Digital downloads, gift cards, and software licenses.
* Intimate apparel and opened hygiene/personal care products.

## Return Process
1. Log into your account and navigate to **Order History** > **Request Return**.
2. Select the item, reason for return, and submit your request.
3. Download and print the pre-paid return shipping label (a $5.99 return postage fee is deducted from non-defective returns).
4. Drop off the package at any authorized carrier drop-off location.

## Defective or Damaged Products
* Defective or damaged items qualify for 100% free return shipping and your choice of an immediate replacement shipment or full refund.
* Claims for defective items must include clear photos or videos showing the issue.

## Refund Timing and Processing
* Once our warehouse inspects and scans the returned parcel, refunds are processed within 3 to 5 business days.
* Depending on your financial institution, credits appear on the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
* Store credit refunds are issued immediately upon return inspection.
